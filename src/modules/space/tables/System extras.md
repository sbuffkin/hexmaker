What else a star system has (Orbits). Roll for each row: it is there when its Chance comes up. A Result is a terrain, or a terrain type (the palette's first terrain of that type). Where: beside the mainworld, or outside (past the last orbit).

| Result | Chance | Where |
|--------|--------|-------|
| starport | always | beside mainworld |
| station | 1 in 2 | beside mainworld |
| jump point | always | outside |
| comet | 6 in 10 | outside |
