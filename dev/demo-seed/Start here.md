# Overworld demo

A sandbox vault. Nothing here touches your real vault. The map view opens on **Demo Bricks**: a 19-hex overworld from the World biomes generator. Every hex opens into an 8 × 8 region, and all 19 regions are already made.

Things to try:

- [ ] **The full view.** The overworld shows every region's hexes stitched together. Hover a hex for its region's name. Click anywhere to go into that region, at that spot.
- [ ] **The minimap** (bottom right). Click a hex on it to jump to that region. Inside a region it outlines where you are.
- [ ] **Cross a seam.** In a region, the names in the bar above the map are its neighbours ("North-east: …"). The dimmed hexes past the edge are the next region: click one to go there.
- [ ] **Find a transition.** Hexes with a white outline (Biomes view, or the minimap) are in-between biomes, like taiga between tundra and forest.
- [ ] **Paint across regions.** On the overworld, pick the terrain tool and paint a river valley or a road corridor straight over the stitched hexes. It writes into each region it crosses. Undo takes the whole stroke back.
- [ ] **Set a biome.** Switch to **Biomes** (bottom right) and click a hex. Pick another biome: the region is remade (it asks first). Your hand-painted hexes stay.
- [ ] **Reroll and tune.** In the same box, **Reroll** for a new layout, or **Settings…** for blend, wiggly borders and seed, with a live preview.
- [ ] **Locations.** The middle hex has three locations: right-click it for "Locations (3)…".
- [ ] **Go up.** From a region, the breadcrumb (or Go up) returns to the overworld and flashes the hex.

Hex-flower regions (a hex of hexes per overworld hex) aren't in this demo yet.
