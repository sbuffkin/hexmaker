Region notes — design choices

Your idea: one note per map holds the per-hex data; hex notes only appear when a hex gets real content. Pick one option per card, add notes, then Submit (Claude is watching for it).

Where things stand in your vault:
  • 9,438 hex notes across 14 maps (chult 2,130 · aaaabab 1,374 · the-coast 960 …). In the-coast, only 686 of 960 differ from the template.
  • Per-hex data today lives in each hex note's frontmatter (terrain, icon, GM icons, region, submap) and 12 source files read it from there.
  • Map-level data (size, palette, paths, overlays, viewport…) lives in the plugin's data.json, not in notes.
